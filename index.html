Video Requirements
Get the Most Out of Your Footage

For the most useful technique feedback, use clear, stable footage where the swimmer remains visible throughout the movement.

FORMAT

MP4 or MOV recommended.

RESOLUTION

720p minimum. 1080p recommended.

FRAME RATE

30 fps minimum. 60 fps recommended when available.

ORIENTATION

Landscape is recommended.

CAMERA

A stable camera or tripod-mounted setup is preferred.

LIGHTING

Use bright, clear footage with minimal glare.

SUBJECT

Keep one swimmer clearly visible throughout the clip.

LENGTH

Use focused clips that contain enough movement to evaluate the technique.

Recommended Camera Angles
Best general-purpose angle

Side View

Position the camera approximately perpendicular to the swimmer's direction.

  • Body alignment
  • Head position
  • Arm movement
  • Hand entry
  • Pull pattern
  • Hip position
  • Kick
  • Body rotation
  • Overall stroke mechanics
Best for underwater mechanics

Underwater Side View

Position the camera underwater and approximately perpendicular to the swimmer.

  • Underwater pull
  • Kick
  • Body position
  • Hand path
  • Streamline
  • Underwater movement
Useful for overall stroke and race footage

Above-Water / Elevated View

Keep the entire swimmer visible and avoid filming from extremely far away.

⚠️ Avoid

  • Shaky handheld footage
  • Extremely dark footage
  • Heavy glare on the water
  • The swimmer being partially out of frame
  • Extremely distant camera positions
  • Excessive zoom
  • Multiple swimmers blocking the subject
  • Footage where the swimmer repeatedly disappears from view
  • Clips that are too short to contain enough movement

For technique analysis, one clearly visible swimmer is much more useful than a crowded pool scene.

Chase provides AI-generated performance insights for informational purposes. It is designed to complement, not replace, qualified coaching and professional guidance.
